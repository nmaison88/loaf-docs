Reading

How does Loaf remember my place?

As you swipe through the feed, Loaf saves the verse you’re on. Each reading plan, including the Whole Bible, keeps its own place, so switching plans never loses your progress. Open the Plans tab to continue any of them.

What is Explore?

When you jump somewhere from search, your saved verses or the chapter picker, you’re exploring. You can scroll freely without moving your reading spot. Tap Back to at the top to return to your plan, or pick up your side trip later from the Explore card on the Plans tab.

How are plan days marked as done?

In a book plan, a day is ticked off automatically when you read its last verse. You can also tick or untick days yourself on the Plans tab.

Can I choose which translations to switch between?

Yes. On the Me tab, switch on the translations you want, and the translation button at the top of the feed cycles through just those.

Listening and reminders

Read aloud isn’t making any sound

Check your volume, and that read aloud is switched on in the Me tab. For more natural voices, download an “Enhanced” or “Premium” voice in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English, then choose it in Loaf.

My daily reminder isn’t arriving

Make sure notifications are allowed for Loaf in Settings → Notifications → Loaf, and that a Focus mode isn’t silencing them. Then turn the reminder off and on again in the Me tab.

Your data

Will I lose my notes if I delete the app?

Yes. Loaf keeps everything on your device and has no account or cloud sync, so deleting the app deletes your likes, saves, notes and progress. Your iPhone backups may include them. See the privacy policy for details.
