Loaf

Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

Loaf doesn’t collect your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

What stays on your device

To remember your place and preferences, Loaf saves the following in its own storage on your iPhone:

  • Your reading position and progress in reading plans
  • Verses you like or save, and the notes you write
  • Your settings, such as translation, text size, appearance, read-aloud voice and reminder time

This information is never sent to us or anyone else. It’s deleted when you delete the app. It may be included in your iPhone’s own backups (iCloud or your computer), which are controlled by your Apple settings.

Daily reminders

If you turn on a daily reminder, the notification is scheduled on your device by iOS. Loaf doesn’t use push notifications or a server, and you can turn reminders off in the app or in iPhone Settings at any time.

Read aloud

Read aloud uses the voices built into your iPhone. Verses are spoken on your device and aren’t sent to us.

Sharing

When you tap Share, Loaf opens the standard iOS share sheet with the verse (and your note, if you choose to share it). You decide which app it goes to; that app’s own privacy policy then applies.

Third parties

Loaf doesn’t share data with third parties, because it doesn’t collect any. The Bible translations are included in the app, so reading doesn’t require an internet connection.

Children

Loaf is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Loaf adds a feature that processes data off your device, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
